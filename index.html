Club Rules

  • Clean up your gaming station after use.

Gaming Terminology Glossary

FPS (Frames Per Second)
A metric measuring frame rate in video games, indicating display smoothness.
NPC (Non-Player Character)
Any character in a game that is not controlled by a human player.
Ping
The network latency between a player's device and the game server, measured in milliseconds.
