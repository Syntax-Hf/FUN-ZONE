Our Story

Founded in 2024, Pixel Realm Gaming Club began as a small group of friends playing multiplayer games after school. Today, it has grown into a vibrant community of passionate gamers!

Our Mission

We aim to connect players of all skill levels, provide state-of-the-art equipment, and host competitive esports tournaments that encourage sportsmanship and community growth.
